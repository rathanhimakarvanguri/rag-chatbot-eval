# Performance and Promotions

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Review Cycle

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, manager assessment and at least two peer reviews.

## Ratings

Ratings use a five-point scale: Exceptional, Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Partially Meets, and Does Not Meet. Ratings are calibrated across each department before they are shared with employees.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided only in the April cycle. To be considered, an employee must have spent at least 12 months in their current level and received Exceeds Expectations or higher in the last review.

## Salary Revisions

Annual salary revisions take effect from 1 July. Employees who join after 1 January are not eligible for that year's revision.

## Performance Improvement Plans

An employee rated Does Not Meet is placed on a performance improvement plan lasting 60 days, with fortnightly check-ins with the manager and an HR partner.
