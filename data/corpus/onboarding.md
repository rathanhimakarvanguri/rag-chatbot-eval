# Onboarding Guide

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Probation

New employees complete a probation period of 6 months. The manager holds a probation review in the fifth month, and probation can be extended once by up to 3 months.

## First Week

On day one, new joiners collect their laptop from the IT desk and complete identity verification. During the first week they must finish the mandatory courses on information security, code of conduct and prevention of sexual harassment.

## Buddy Programme

Each new joiner is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for the first 90 days. Buddies meet the new joiner at least once a week.

## Payroll Setup

Bank details must be added in the HR portal by the 20th of the joining month to be included in that month's payroll. Salaries are credited on the last working day of each month.

## Notice Period

During probation the notice period is 30 days for either side. After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days, or 90 days for roles at manager level and above.
