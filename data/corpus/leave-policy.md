# Leave Policy

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees earn 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Employees on probation accrue leave but can only use it after completing probation. Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 March.

Annual leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance. Shorter requests need 2 working days of notice.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave

Birth mothers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Other parents, including adoptive parents and partners, receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees can take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family means a spouse, child, parent, sibling or grandparent.

## Public Holidays

The company publishes a list of 12 public holidays each December for the following year. Employees may swap up to 2 of these holidays for other festivals of their choice, provided the swap is logged in the HR portal before the holiday.
