# Benefits Overview

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Health Insurance

Group health insurance covers the employee, spouse, up to two children and two parents or parents-in-law. The sum insured is INR 5 lakh per family per year. Employees can buy a top-up of a further INR 10 lakh at group rates during the enrolment window in April.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, certifications, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Certifications costing more than INR 40,000 can be funded if the employee signs a 12-month retention agreement.

## Wellness

The company reimburses gym or sports memberships up to INR 1,000 per month. Every employee also has free access to a confidential employee assistance programme with counselling sessions.

## Referral Bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus of INR 50,000, paid after the new hire completes 3 months. Referral bonuses are not paid for referring candidates into one's own reporting line.

## Meals

Office locations provide free lunch on office days. Employees working past 21:00 in the office can expense dinner up to INR 400.
