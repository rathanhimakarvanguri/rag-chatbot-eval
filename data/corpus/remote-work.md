# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Hybrid Schedule

The default schedule is hybrid: employees work from the office on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays, and may work remotely on Mondays and Fridays. Teams may agree on a different set of three office days with their manager's written approval.

## Fully Remote Roles

A role can be designated fully remote only by a department head. Fully remote employees must still attend the office for the quarterly planning week, and the company reimburses their travel for it.

## Working From Another City

Employees may work from another city within India for up to 30 days per calendar year without special approval. Working from outside India requires prior approval from both HR and the Information Security team, and is limited to 15 days per year.

## Home Office Allowance

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through the expense tool within 60 days of joining.

## Core Hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Employees should be available for meetings during core hours regardless of where they work.
