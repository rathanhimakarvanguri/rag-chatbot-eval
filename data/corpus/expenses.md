# Travel and Expense Policy

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Submitting Expenses

All expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days of being incurred, with an itemised receipt for anything above INR 500. Claims submitted after 90 days are rejected.

## Approval Limits

Managers can approve individual claims up to INR 50,000. Claims above INR 50,000 also need approval from the finance controller.

## Air Travel

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel desk. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy. Flights should be booked at least 10 days before travel unless the trip is urgent.

## Hotels and Meals

The hotel limit is INR 7,000 per night in metro cities and INR 5,000 per night elsewhere in India. The daily meal allowance during travel is INR 1,500. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Local Commute

Cab rides for client visits are reimbursed at actual cost. Employees who use a personal car for work travel are reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre.

## Corporate Card

Employees who travel more than 4 times a quarter can request a corporate card. The corporate card must not be used for personal spending, and statements must be reconciled within 15 days of the billing date.
