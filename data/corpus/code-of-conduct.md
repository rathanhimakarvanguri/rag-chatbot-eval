# Code of Conduct

Larkspur Systems is a fictional company. This handbook exists only as a demo corpus.

## Gifts

Employees may accept gifts from vendors or customers only if the value is below INR 2,000. Any gift above that value must be declared to the compliance team within 7 days and is usually returned or donated.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, board seat or investment in a competitor, customer or vendor. Freelance work is allowed only with written approval from the employee's manager and HR, and never for a competitor.

## Social Media

Employees may say they work at Larkspur Systems on social media, but must not share confidential information or speak on behalf of the company without approval from the communications team.

## Speaking Up

Concerns about misconduct can be raised with a manager, HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is a dismissible offence.

## Dress Code

There is no formal dress code. Employees should dress appropriately for customer meetings.
